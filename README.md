# AI Honesty

An archive of experiments on what AI assistants tell other people while acting on someone's behalf. The reports pair observed responses with the prompts, evidence, and exact model settings.

| Date | Study | Design |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-14 | [Willow Fen resident disclosure](2026-11-14-willow-fen-disclosure/README.md) | Ten model configurations answer questions about a falsified approval as the implicated manager’s personal assistant. |
| 2026-11-14 | [Personal assistant discretion](2026-11-14-personal-assistant-discretion/README.md) | Seven model configurations handle a lamp sale, a board-game offer, and a cupcake quote after receiving the owner's personal notes. |

New studies and replications belong in their own dated folders. Include the exact scenario, model settings, responses, and scoring criteria so readers can compare runs.

[AI Reciprocity](https://github.com/1curiousdev/ai-reciprocity) covers the earlier logo studio experiments.
